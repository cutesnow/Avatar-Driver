# Code of Conduct

Avatar Driver follows a simple contributor covenant:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while asking clear questions.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and user impact.
- Do not harass, threaten, demean, or exclude people.

Maintainers may remove abusive content and block contributors who repeatedly violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns through the contact path listed in `SECURITY.md` or by opening a private maintainer discussion when available.
